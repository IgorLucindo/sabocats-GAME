# SaboCats — Lore & Narrative Context

## 1. Setting: The Rodent Syndicate
The city is controlled from the sewers to the rooftops by the Mafia Rat — a ruthless, impatient criminal mastermind who runs local protection rackets, illegal smuggling, and black-market operations.

---

## 2. Origin: The Ambush
A crew of stray alley cats were living quiet neighborhood lives until they were ambushed in their home alleyway by the Mafia Rat and his enforcers. The Rat presented a single ultimatum: work for him, or disappear. The cats are now permanently on his payroll.

---

## 3. The Match Narrative
- **The Job:** For each match, the Mafia Rat targets a debtor, rival, or asset. He sends his feline crew into the area simultaneously.
- **The Impatience Timer (2 Minutes):** The Mafia Rat does not tolerate slow work. The match timer represents the Rat losing his patience. He watches closely, issuing threats as time ticks down.
- **Lives & Thresholds:** The Rat usually demands perfection, but on certain jobs (configured by the host's `lives` and `pointsToWin` settings), he may allow the cats a few failures before the syndicate steps in.
- **Single Survivor Protocol:** The Rat refuses loose ends. Only the cat who survives and completes the job gets the reward. The rest answer to the Mafia.

---

## 4. Narrative Vignettes & Integrations

### 4.1 The Tutorial Sequence
The game opens with the Mafia Rat actively angering the player. The player chases the Rat through a city alley, organically learning movement mechanics (jumping, wall-sliding) through environmental hints. The chase ends when the Rat — flanked by his henchmen — officially invites the player to do "missions" for him, introducing the world globe map selector.

### 4.2 The Enforcers
The Mafia Rat relies on two massive human henchmen. In reality, both men are completely hollow and controlled directly by smaller rats nested beneath their caps, pulling hair and barking muffled orders (a la *Ratatouille*). 

### 4.3 The Alleyway Stalker
While traversing the lobby alleyway, players catch a fleeting, one-time glimpse of the Mafia Rat’s pure black silhouette dashing across overhead power cables.

### 4.4 Mission Transitions & Spawns
When a map is selected at the globe, players lose control (forced movement) and are corralled by the Rat and his henchmen into cages. The henchmen slam the cages shut aggressively, angering the cats. When the round begins on the map, a cinematic plays showing the cats being released from these cages into the operational zone.
